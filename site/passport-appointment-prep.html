US passport appointment prep

A free checklist using official tools only. If slots are scarce, widen your search — do not use unofficial scrapers.

Open USPS RCAS Join waitlist

Before you book

  1. Check processing times at travel.state.gov.
  2. Confirm whether you need an appointment (many renewals by mail do not).
  3. Complete DS-11 / supporting docs; bring photo and payment if you go in person.
  4. Schedule only via USPS RCAS or a participating Post Office.

If no slots appear

  1. Widen radius and try another office.
  2. Retry at off-peak hours; inventory changes.
  3. Avoid third-party scrapers that violate site terms.
  4. Use the Waitlist if you want a future policy-compliant alert option — commerce is off today.

Honest scope

Waitwise is a Mason Ventures public experiment. We publish free guides and a demand-signal ledger. We do not scrape government schedulers and we do not sell access.
