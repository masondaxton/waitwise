Open USPS RCAS Waitlist (passport)

Before you book

  1. Check processing times at travel.state.gov.
  2. Confirm whether you need an appointment — many renewals by mail do not.
  3. Complete DS-11 / supporting docs; bring photo and payment if you go in person.
  4. Schedule only via USPS RCAS or a participating Post Office.

If no slots appear

  1. Widen radius and try another office.
  2. Retry at off-peak hours — cancellations can flash open for minutes.
  3. Avoid third-party scrapers that violate site terms.
  4. If you want a future policy-compliant alert option, comment passport on the waitlist. Commerce is off today — we are not sending texts.
