USPS passport appointment not available?

If Retail Customer Appointment Scheduler (RCAS) shows no nearby slots — or keeps failing — use the official path below. This page does not scrape USPS and does not sell alerts.

What people usually mean

  • “No appointments available” near my ZIP
  • Calendar days look open but times will not select
  • “This service is currently unavailable” on the scheduler
  • Local office cancelled passport appointments

Do this next (official)

  1. Open the USPS RCAS scheduler during the post office’s business hours; try another browser if booking stalls.
  2. Search by location / ZIP, then widen radius and try nearby cities — scarcity is often local, not national.
  3. Check non-USPS acceptance facilities (libraries, clerks of court, some colleges). State Dept locator: iafdb.travel.state.gov.
  4. Ask the local Post Office about walk-in passport hours or lobby kiosk scheduling.
  5. Confirm whether you can renew by mail instead of a new in-person appointment: travel.state.gov renew.
  6. If travel is within ~14 days, use State Dept urgent guidance / agency appointments — not third-party scrapers.

Honest map of alternatives

  • Official: USPS RCAS, State Dept processing times, acceptance facility locator.
  • Paid alert products (not us): services such as PassportAlerts advertise SMS/email when slots open. Commerce is OFF here — we do not take payment.
  • Avoid: government-site scrapers and “guaranteed appointment” claims.
