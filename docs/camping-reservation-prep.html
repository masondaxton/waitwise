Who this is for

Campers who keep missing Recreation.gov (and similar) release windows — and anyone comparing paid alert tools vs a honest prep path.

Night-before checklist

  1. Confirm the park’s release calendar on Recreation.gov (or the official state-park booker).
  2. Create/login to your official account; verify email and payment method work.
  3. Write down party size, vehicle length, and preferred loops/sites.
  4. Set two calendar alarms: 10 minutes before release and at release time (timezone of the park).

At release

  1. Open only the official booking site — avoid third-party “finder” tabs that confuse checkout.
  2. Search your pre-chosen park + dates first; have a backup park ready.
  3. If inventory is empty, widen dates/loops once — then stop thrashing.
  4. Paid alert products exist; treat review claims as claims. Waitwise does not automate booking.

Meaningful next step

If a free, policy-compliant alert option would help later, comment on the waitlist with your park type (national / state / private). Commerce is OFF today.
